ClipBot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

ClipBot is a private, self-hosted tool operated by Jonas (the "Operator"). It runs on the Operator's own computer. It turns short clips from Twitch channels whose owners have given permission into vertical videos and publishes them to the Operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

ClipBot is not offered to the public. The only person who signs in to ClipBot with TikTok is the Operator.

Contact: joni1245780@gmail.com

2. What data ClipBot processes

2.1 Data from TikTok (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API)

When the Operator connects their TikTok account, ClipBot receives and stores:

  • an access token and a refresh token,
  • the TikTok account identifier (open_id),
  • the list of granted permissions (scopes), and
  • before each post, the creator information TikTok returns for the connected account (for example the allowed privacy levels and the maximum video length).

ClipBot uses this data only to upload and publish videos to the Operator's own TikTok account and to show the posting options TikTok allows for that account. ClipBot does not read, collect or store data about other TikTok users, followers, comments, messages or viewing behaviour.

2.2 Video content

ClipBot processes video clips from Twitch channels listed in its configuration. Clips include the image and voice of the respective streamers. ClipBot only processes channels whose owners have given permission. Each published video names the original streamer. To create subtitles, the audio is transcribed locally on the Operator's computer; no audio is sent to a transcription service.

2.3 Other services

  • Twitch API: to find clips of the configured channels (public clip data such as title, view count and duration).
  • YouTube Data API (Google): to upload the same videos to the Operator's YouTube channel.
  • Telegram Bot API: to send each finished video to the Operator for approval before it is published.
  • Anthropic API (optional): may receive a clip's title, streamer name, game and duration to suggest a video title and description. No video or audio is sent.

3. Where data is stored and for how long

  • TikTok tokens are stored in a local file on the Operator's computer. They are not sent anywhere except to TikTok. The refresh token expires after 180 days or when access is revoked.
  • Processed video files are stored locally and deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • A local database keeps technical records (clip ID, processing status, IDs of the published posts) to avoid publishing the same clip twice.

ClipBot does not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone except the services listed above, and only for the purposes described.

4. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (wording may vary), or by contacting the Operator. After revocation, ClipBot can no longer post and the stored tokens become invalid; the Operator deletes them on request.
